Yeah…
Back when the world moved slow, when the sunlight cut bright.
Dreams packed in pockets, hearts kicking real light.
Before the noise, before the weight, before the late-night fight—
We lived in moments that cracked…
Then kicked straight back to life.

And the memories rush in…

I remember bike rides under orange skies
Dirty sneakers, playground dreams in our eyes
Mama yelling from the window “come inside”
But we stayed outside till the streetlights died
Old cartoons playing on a broken TV
Back when happiness was feeling truly free
No stress, no bills, no fear of losing time
Now my phone keeps buzzing while I’m losing mine
We wanted fast cars, money, and respect
Now we got responsibilities stacked on our necks
Funny how we rushed just to grow up fast
Now every grown soul wanna go back

Take me back to the slow-mo days
Where the world felt wide and deep
Where a cardboard box was a silver ship
And the promises were easy to keep
Now we’re chasing ghosts in a neon rain
Just to feel like we’re still alive
But that little kid with the messy hair
Is the only reason I survive.

Grandma’s house smelled like coffee and old wood
Little moments back then felt so good
Running through the hallways playing hide and seek
Now we hide emotions every single week
Back when friendship meant knocking on the door
Now conversations feel artificial more and more
Everybody scrolling, everybody numb
Looking for connection through a glowing sun
And sometimes when the rain hits the window pane
I can hear my younger self call my name
Saying “don’t you let the fire lose its glow”
Before you lose the kid you used to know.

Maybe growing up… was never meant to kill the kid inside
Maybe life just taught us how to survive
But tonight…
I just wanna breathe again.

Where the world felt wide and deep
Where a cardboard box was a silver ship
And the promises were easy to keep
Now we’re chasing ghosts in a neon rain
Just to feel like we’re still alive
But that little kid with the messy hair

We grew older…
But those memories still live somewhere in the static…
Somewhere between the silence…
And the music.
